# Stereo Pocket Radio Enclosure

3D-printable STL files for the Stereo Pocket Radio Receiver 76-108MHz DIY soldering kit.

## Printing Instructions

- **Material:** PLA or PETG (File 2), TPU (File 3)
- **Layer Height:** 0.2mm
- **Infill:** 15% – 20%
- **Supports:** Not required
- **Walls:** 2–4

## Included Files

1. `PLA Radio Casing and TPU Membrane` – File containing all prints together
2. `PLA Radio Casing` – Main casing for print
3. `TPU Radio Membrane` – Covers pushbuttons

## Assembly Instructions

1. **Attach Membrane:** Glue the TPU membrane to the underside of the front casing (the plate labeled with Channel, Power, Volume, etc.), making sure the buttons are aligned.
2. **Insert Receiver:** Slide the assembled 76-108MHz Radio Receiver PCB into the main hollow rectangular casing until it hits the retaining notches, ensuring the audio port lines up with the hole.
3. **Attach Front Cover:** Align the front casing (from Step 1) over the circuit board and push until it snaps/fits firmly against the internal notches.
4. **Secure Back Cover:** Place the battery casing over the back battery pack and secure it using glue or small screws.
